About

I am an Assistant Professor at the School of Studies in Computer Science & IT, Pt. Ravishankar Shukla University, Raipur, and a PhD researcher in the Department of Computer Applications at NIT Raipur. My research focuses on federated learning, semantic communication, edge-deployable lightweight language models, and adversarial robustness in distributed Industrial IoT (IIoT) systems.

FedSec-SemanticIoT — PhD Thesis Framework

Ongoing

A federated, privacy-preserving and edge-deployable semantic sensing framework for IIoT. It addresses four gaps: dependence on a central LLM, lack of adversarial robustness, no edge deployability, and missing communication-overhead analysis. Planned validation uses benchmarks including PAMAP2, whuGAIT and FER-2013.
